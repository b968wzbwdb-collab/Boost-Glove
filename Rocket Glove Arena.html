HOW TO PLAY

DRIVE

Push the left stick left or right to drive. Let go to roll to a stop. Hold the opposite way a moment to turn the car around.

BOOST

Hold BOOST. Two rockets under the car push it out of its roof side. The meter refills when you let go. There is no jump button.

AIR CONTROL

The same stick works in the air. It points the nose, so tilt the nose down to fly forward. Left and right also nudge you sideways.

PUNCH

The spring glove shoots out and launches the ball or knocks the rival back. It recharges for exactly 3 seconds.

SCORE

The whole ball must pass through the other ring. Player 1 scores in the right ring, Player 2 in the left. Most goals after 2:30 wins.

KEYBOARD

P1: W A S D, Space boost, F punch. P2: arrow keys, Enter boost, / punch. Against the CPU the arrow keys work for P1 too.
